Subject: Demo invitation: OT Platform for security visibility and operational risk

Dear colleagues,

I would like to demonstrate the OT Platform, a single workspace for viewing operational technology assets, vulnerabilities, threat context, audit evidence, and risk. The goal is to make it easier to understand what is exposed, what evidence supports that view, and what action or owner is needed next.

Why it matters
Security information is often spread across OT asset tools, SIEMs, vulnerability systems, ticketing platforms, and spreadsheets. That fragmentation slows triage and makes leadership reporting harder. The platform brings supported data and local workflows together, while identifying whether information is live, sample, unavailable, or simply configured but unverified.

What the demo will cover
- OT device inventory and connectivity, with Claroty xDome live feeds when configured.
- Vulnerability and CVE exposure, risk prioritization, and threat-intelligence context.
- Alert/event search and an observable pivot across local indicators, known-exploited data, and stored sightings.
- Audit assignments, workbook evidence, revision history, and exportable reports.
- SIEM evidence, Zero Trust readiness, automation dry-runs, and an automation ROI view.

Integration coverage
Rubrik Security Cloud supports read-only activity/audit polling and an inbound webhook. Manual read-only API sync is available for GitHub, GitLab, MISP, Keycloak, OpenSearch, Wazuh Indexer, SentinelOne, Microsoft Defender for Cloud Apps, Prisma Cloud, NetApp ONTAP, and Cato auditFeed. Webhook, REST JSON, and syslog HTTP-bridge connectors can also receive events. Other vendor cards may store encrypted settings without yet providing live polling.

What can be automated
The platform can refresh configured feeds, normalize and route events into local evidence, check inbound indicators against a local threat index, maintain audit workbook revisions, generate reports/exports, and record playbook dry-runs. These capabilities reduce repetitive collection and evidence assembly.

Important boundary
This is a visibility and workflow tool, not an autonomous response system. Playbooks are dry-runs; Zero Trust decisions are simulations; IP lists do not enforce firewall rules; and Snort rules are catalogued but not deployed or executed. Some analytics are sample data. Saved credentials alone do not prove connectivity, so the demo will distinguish verified sources from unconfigured or unavailable ones.

For the next step, I recommend selecting a pilot site and data owner, then approving least-privilege read access for the first integrations.

Cyber Security Audit Guidance
Cyber security audits are divided by who performs them and what they evaluate. Use this page as an operational checklist when selecting the right audit style for your industry and control objective.
Automotive Domain Guidelines
  • Map ECUs, gateways, telematics, diagnostics, manufacturing systems, cloud services, and supplier dependencies in scope.
  • Review secure boot, signed firmware, software provenance, vulnerability handling, and end-of-support risks.
  • Assess OTA update authorization, package signing, staged rollout, rollback, and recovery procedures.
  • Check segmentation and access controls for CAN/CAN-FD, LIN, automotive Ethernet, and diagnostic interfaces.
  • Review vehicle, backend, and supplier identity, certificate, and cryptographic key lifecycle controls.
  • Trace component vulnerabilities and software bills of materials; coordinate cyber changes with functional safety review.
  • Map evidence to ISO/SAE 21434 and UNECE R155/R156 where applicable.
OT Security Guidelines
  • Inventory OT assets by process, criticality, owner, connectivity, and support status.
  • Review IT/OT segmentation, zones and conduits, approved communications, and boundary controls.
  • Restrict remote access through approved, monitored jump paths with strong authentication and time-bounded access.
  • Assess engineering workstation hardening, authorized changes, configuration backups, and tested restoration.
  • Prefer passive monitoring where active testing could affect process safety or availability.
  • Exercise incident response with operations and safety teams, including safe-state and manual recovery procedures.
  • Map controls to ISA/IEC 62443 and NIST SP 800-82 as appropriate; authorize testing before execution.
Healthcare Domain
  • Audit ePHI safeguards for encryption, storage, transmission, and monitoring.
  • Review access logs so only care teams view patient records they are authorized to access.
  • Inspect connected medical devices such as pacemakers, insulin pumps, scanners, and other clinical equipment.
  • Align evidence with HIPAA-focused control expectations.
Financial Sector
  • Evaluate tools that support wire transfers, ACH, trading, and high-value payment workflows.
  • Test fraud-detection controls for unusual transaction spikes and account misuse.
  • Review third-party vendor and fintech dependencies as part of the audit boundary.
  • Map requirements to GLBA, FFIEC, SOC 2, and related financial governance controls.
Critical Infrastructure & OT
  • Audit operational technology that controls industrial or physical processes.
  • Verify air-gapping, segmentation, and remote-access restrictions for high-impact assets.
  • Assess SCADA platforms and engineering workstations for cyber-physical resilience.
  • Use NIST Cybersecurity Framework baselines to measure control coverage and risk treatment.
